# EC Market Research

Use the EC Pulse MCP tools to gather current marketplace evidence before making conclusions.

## Workflow

1. Clarify the product/category and target marketplace when needed.
2. Use `ec_product_search` to discover candidates.
3. Use `ec_product_get` or `ec_product_compare` for concrete product evidence.
4. Use `ec_research_ingest` when public review/comment URLs are available.
5. Use `ec_consumer_insights` for customer pain points and product angles.
6. Present source URLs, prices, marketplace, and uncertainty separately from your interpretation.
7. Do not invent missing prices, ratings, inventory, or market share.

## Opportunity analysis

When asked for opportunities, combine product evidence with customer pain points. Distinguish observed signals from hypotheses that require validation.
